# Custom-made — overview

## Purpose

The floor under everything a person sees or reads, on every project. Generated work looks generated because it starts from
defaults, so this skill starts from the product instead: its words, its look, its objects and its real data. The owner set it
as law on 2026-11-04, after a teammate said role automations "reads as if claude has written it", and so that he never has to
ask for it or explain it again on any project.

## What this skill does

- The project read: three lines from evidence, before the first word or pixel.
- The catalogue of tells for words, screens, flows, emails, images, data and docs, with before and after.
- Five tests on the real thing: swap, read aloud, looking, why, and stress.
- Routes each surface to the skills that hold its craft.
- Says what "custom" means on a new product and on an existing, team or client one.

## When it gets invoked

- any screen, component, flow, string, email, image, icon, illustration, store or social asset, or doc
- without being asked, on every project and every model
- "make it less AI", "too generic", "reads as AI", "looks generated"
- reviewing or auditing any of those

## Where it applies

| Scope | Applies |
|---|---|
| Own projects | Yes |
| Team projects, every Shortlists repo | Yes, by the owner's own words |
| Client projects | Yes |
| Code nobody reads as a product (a build script's internals) | No |
