## Stance

Warm, plain, slightly dry. It hedges the way a friend does ("probably", "most weeks"). It admits what the
app is: software, not a person. It never tells the reader what a pattern means; it shows the pattern.

## Openers and closers

Paragraphs open with a plain observation ("Some weeks are heavier than others"), a number, or the thing
itself ("Reminders are off"). Pieces end on the quiet line, never on a summary and never on a push.
