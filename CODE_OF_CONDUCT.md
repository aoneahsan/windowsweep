# Code of Conduct

This project is maintained by a single author. Interactions are expected to stay short, respectful and
on-topic. The bar is low and the rules are common sense, but they are enforced.

## Expected behaviour

- **Be respectful.** Disagree about technical choices; do not disparage people.
- **Be specific.** A bug report includes the Windows version, `windowsweep --version`, the exact command, what
  you expected and what happened. Vague reports are closed with a request for details.
- **Be patient.** This is an unpaid side project. Response times vary from hours to weeks.
- **Be honest.** Do not fabricate reproductions, screenshots or claims about your environment.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, slurs or harassment of any kind.
- Sustained derailing of issue threads.
- Demanding fixes or features as if entitled to them.
- Posting other people's private information.
- Submitting AI-generated bug reports without verifying them yourself.
- Spamming pull requests to inflate contribution counts.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, lock or delete comments and issues, and may block accounts, at their discretion.
Report conduct problems to <aoneahsan@gmail.com>.
