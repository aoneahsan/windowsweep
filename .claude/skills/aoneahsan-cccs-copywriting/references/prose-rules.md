# Prose rules — the Elements of Style, distilled

Strunk's composition rules (1918, public domain), with before/after examples. Apply to any prose a human
reads. The full 12k-token text is not vendored here — this is the working subset.

## Rule 13 — Omit needless words (the master rule)

Vigorous writing is concise. Every word should tell.

| Wordy | Tight |
|---|---|
| the reason is that | because |
| in order to | to |
| owing to the fact that | because / since |
| there is no doubt that | doubtless / no doubt |
| this is a subject that | this subject |
| the question as to whether | whether |
| he is a man who | he |
| used for fuel purposes | used for fuel |

## Rule 10 — Use the active voice

More direct and vigorous than the passive.

- ❌ There were a great number of dead leaves lying on the ground.
- ✅ Dead leaves covered the ground.
- ❌ The request is handled by the worker. → ✅ The worker handles the request.

Exception: the passive is right when the actor is unknown or irrelevant ("The build was cancelled").

## Rule 11 — Put statements in positive form

Assert. Avoid tame, colorless, hesitating "not".

- ❌ did not remember → ✅ forgot
- ❌ did not pay attention to → ✅ ignored
- ❌ not honest → ✅ dishonest
- ❌ not important → ✅ trifling

## Rule 12 — Use definite, specific, concrete language

The surest way to hold a reader's attention.

- ❌ A period of unfavorable weather set in. → ✅ It rained every day for a week.
- ❌ improves performance → ✅ cuts cold-start first paint from 3.1s to 0.8s

## Rule 16 — Keep related words together

Position shows relationship; separation confuses.

- ❌ He noticed a large stain in the rug that was right in the center.
- ✅ He noticed a large stain right in the center of the rug.
- Keep subject and verb close; keep a modifier next to what it modifies.

## Rule 18 — Place the emphatic words at the end

The proper place for the word the writer most wants to stress is the end of the sentence.

- ❌ This steel is principally used for making razors, because of its hardness.
- ✅ Because of its hardness, this steel is used principally for making razors.

## Rules 8–9 — Paragraphs

- One topic per paragraph; start a new paragraph on each new topic.
- Begin each paragraph with a topic sentence that states its point; the rest supports it.

## Rule 15 — Express co-ordinate ideas in similar form (parallelism)

- ❌ Formerly, science was taught by the textbook method, while now the laboratory method is employed.
- ✅ Formerly, science was taught by the textbook method; now it is taught by the laboratory method.
- In lists and headings, keep every item the same grammatical shape.

## Rule 14 & 17 — Rhythm and tense

- Avoid a long run of loose sentences all joined by "and/but/so"; vary the structure.
- In a summary, keep to one tense — don't drift between past and present.

## The two-pass habit

1. Draft using judgment.
2. Edit: cut every needless word, flip passive to active, make vague words concrete, move the point to the
   end of the sentence. Read it once more aloud in your head — if you stumble, the reader will too.
