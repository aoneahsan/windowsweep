## Screen 1 — You

Some weeks are heavier than others. Steady counts the entries you make, and it never counts the days you
don't, so a Tuesday with nothing in it is a gap on the week view and nothing more.

**See an entry**

## Screen 2 — What changes

No red number waiting for you on Wednesday morning. Every entry is one line: what you did, how it felt.
Six words is plenty. By Sunday the week view has a shape (the kettle before the reading, the long
Thursdays).

**See reminders**

## Screen 3 — Reminders, if you want them

Steady won't say what that means, or nudge you. Reminders are off unless you pick a time and your quiet
hours; then it says "Today is open," nothing else. Some days you won't open Steady. That's fine. It'll be
here.

**Choose a reminder** · **Start a week**
