# Surface — CV / resume and job-application answers

This is the owner's own voice (`aoneahsan-cccs-story-personal-voice`), and the one surface where a
plausible-sounding invention does real damage. Every fact about experience comes from him; a gap is a
`NEEDS DECISION`, never a guess.

## The bullet — Freytag in miniature

Situation → action → measured result, in one line:

- verb first, past tense, specific ("Cut cold-start from 3.1 s to 0.8 s on a 2019 Android by …")
- a number with a unit wherever one exists; where none exists, the concrete artefact ("shipped the Play
  Store submission for three apps")
- no adjectives about oneself ("passionate", "results-driven"); the result is the adjective
- no "responsible for", no "helped with" — say what was done
- one line, ≤ 25 words; two lines only for the headline achievement of a role

## Sections and order

Header (name, one-line role, contact, one link) · summary (≤ 3 lines, in the owner's fingerprint — the
only place his rhythm shows) · experience (reverse chronological, 3–5 bullets per recent role, fewer for
older) · projects (only the ones with a live link and a measurable claim) · skills (a plain list, grouped,
no ratings) · education. Standard section names — an applicant-tracking system parses headings, and
cleverness there costs the interview.

## Tailoring

Mirror the job ad's nouns where they are honestly true; reorder bullets so the relevant ones lead; never add
a technology he has not used. The tailored file is a copy; the master stays complete.

## Cover letter and application answers

PAS in three short paragraphs: their problem as the ad states it · what he has done about that exact
problem elsewhere (one specific) · what he would do first in this role. In his voice: contractions if he
uses them, his sentence rhythm, no template phrases. Length ≤ 250 words. A "why us?" answer names one real
thing about the company that is verifiable, never flattery.

## The rules that do not bend

- No overclaim. A stretched title, an inflated number, a solo credit for team work — each is a
  `NEEDS DECISION` to him, in his words, before the draft.
- No AI tells: the banned list applies (`humanize.md`); a CV that reads generated is discarded on sight.
- He edits every draft before it goes anywhere; nothing is sent or finalized by the agent.
- No day-level dates. A CV carries month and year only, and never several launches a few days apart, unless
  he gave those dates himself (owner directive 2026-09-17).
- An app is "on Google Play" only when `aoneahsan-cccs-store-presence` says so, and then it is linked.
- Created and led, never currently running. The CV says what he built and that he is available now — no
  "I run", "my own", "Under my own name", "Founder" (owner directive 2026-09-17).
- Done-only. An unfinished capability is left out, not described: no "in progress", "pending",
  "internal testing", "release candidate". It goes in when he says it shipped. Both gated by
  `career-docs` → `uv run cvgen check`.

## Export

PDF and DOCX from one model, real selectable text, linear reading order for parsers, one to two pages
(`aoneahsan-cccs-document-export`).
