# Surface — therapy, mental health, and any anxious audience

Everything in `safety.md` applies first. This file is the writing shape on top of it.

## Structure

Kishōtenketsu (`frameworks.md`): introduction → development → a shift of perspective → reconciliation.
No enemy, no battle metaphors, no "beat", "fight", "crush", "win". A habit app for anxious students does
not "conquer procrastination"; it shows a day, a missed day, a different way of seeing the missed day, and
the next morning.

## Tone

Warm-competent, slow. Sentences a little longer than elsewhere, then one short one. No exclamation marks.
Second person, present tense. Humor off; warmth stays; the one permitted lightness is gentle self-awareness
about the app ("we are an app, and an app is not a person").

## Language table

| Say | Do not say |
|---|---|
| a missed day · a gap | failed · lapsed · broke your streak |
| you can · when you want · if it helps | you must · you need to · don't forget |
| notice · track · reflect | fix · cure · treat · reduce your anxiety |
| a small step · one entry | crush it · level up · win the day |
| talk to someone you trust · here is a line you can call | you're not alone (alone, as a slogan) |
| this works alongside professional care | this replaces therapy |

## Onboarding for an anxious reader

- Screen 1 names the situation without diagnosing it ("Some weeks are heavier than others").
- Screen 2 says what the app does and does not do, in the same sentence.
- Screen 3 offers reminders as opt-in, with quiet hours and the off switch shown.
- A line, early: "Some days you will not open this. That is fine. It will be here."
- No streak counter on the first screen; if the product has one, it counts entries, not consecutive days.

## Crisis line placement

In settings, in any low-mood entry flow, and on the "about" screen — verified for the locale, recorded in
the Bible, never invented (`safety.md`). The line is offered, not pushed: "If things feel unsafe right now,
988 (US) is available by call or text."

## Claims

No clinical outcome, no percentage of improvement, no "evidence-based" without the evidence named. The
store listing follows the same rule.

## Data

Every string that could contain what the reader wrote is health data: no logging of it, no analytics event
carrying it, no error message quoting it (`aoneahsan-cccs-phi-safe-engineering`).

## The reviewer's hard flags

A joke · a shame or urgency line · a missing or unverified crisis line · a diagnosis or outcome claim · a
battle metaphor · a notification that fires during quiet hours.

Related: `aoneahsan-cccs-reminders-notifications` (opt-in, quiet hours, never an exact alarm) ·
`aoneahsan-cccs-social-community` (if the product carries a community: blocking and reporting are
prerequisites, not features).
