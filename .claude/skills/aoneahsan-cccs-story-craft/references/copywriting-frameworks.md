# Copywriting frameworks — persuasion structures and the awareness ladder

These are structures for a page or a sequence that has to move a reader to act. Clarity and honesty rules
(omit needless words, no fabricated proof, the store banned-word list) come from `aoneahsan-cccs-copywriting`
and apply underneath every one of these.

## Start with the reader's awareness (Schwartz, *Breakthrough Advertising*)

Eugene Schwartz's five levels decide where a piece may begin. Lead with what the reader already knows; a
piece that opens one level above the reader loses them.

| Level | The reader | Open with | Typical surface |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unaware | Does not know they have the problem | A story or an observation about their life, no product | Blog, social |
| Problem-aware | Feels the pain, has no name for a fix | The problem, named precisely (PAS) | Landing hero, email 1 |
| Solution-aware | Knows fixes exist, not yours | The mechanism that makes yours different | Feature page, comparison |
| Product-aware | Knows you, undecided | Proof, specifics, the objection answered | Pricing, case study |
| Most aware | Ready | The offer, the button and the one consent line (*"By continuing, you agree to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy."*), nothing else — never a refund or warning sentence (`payment-copy.md`) | Checkout, upgrade prompt |

The Story Bible records the audience's level; the content map records it per surface.

## AIDA — Attention, Interest, Desire, Action

The oldest one. Attention is the hook (one line, specific); Interest is the reader's situation reflected
back; Desire is the after-state made concrete; Action is one verb. Fits a landing hero, a store listing's
first paragraph, a short ad. Fails when Desire is a list of features instead of a picture of the reader's
day.

## PAS — Problem, Agitate, Solve

Name the problem in the reader's words. Agitate: what it costs them, specifically (time, money, a feeling,
a missed thing) — without manufacturing panic, which the safety reference forbids on anxious audiences.
Solve: the mechanism, then the offer. Fits pain-led products, email sequences, feature pages, README
openers ("you have this problem; here is the tool"). Keep Agitate to two sentences; more reads as
manipulation.

## BAB — Before, After, Bridge

Before: the reader's current state. After: the same scene once the problem is gone. Bridge: how they get
from one to the other (the product, in one sentence). Fits onboarding ("what changes for you"), testimonial
framing, empty states that show what the screen will look like once it has data.

## 4Ps — Promise, Picture, Proof, Push

Promise: the one transformation. Picture: show it happening. Proof: a real number, a named person, a
screenshot — or nothing (never an invented figure). Push: the action, with the reason to act now stated
honestly (a real limit, not a fake countdown). Fits sales pages, pricing pages, upgrade emails.

## StoryBrand (Miller, SB7)

A **character** (the customer) has a **problem**, meets a **guide** (the product, with empathy and
authority), who gives them a **plan**, **calls them to action**, helps them **avoid failure**, and ends in
**success**. The rule that matters: the customer is the hero, the product is the guide. Copy that makes the
product the hero ("we are proud to announce") reads as self-regard. Fits homepages end to end — one section
per beat.

## Which one where

| Surface | Default | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage | StoryBrand | one beat per section, the customer stays the hero |
| Landing hero, store listing | AIDA | short, one action |
| Email sequence | PAS across the sequence, 4Ps on the last email | pain first, proof and push at the end |
| Onboarding, empty states | BAB | the after-state is the whole point of the screen |
| Pricing, case study | 4Ps | proof carries it |
| README opener | PAS in two sentences | then plain reference prose |

## Two traps

- Proof you do not have. A framework slot labelled "Proof" is not permission to invent a statistic. Real
  proof or the slot is skipped — `aoneahsan-cccs-copywriting`.
- Agitation on the wrong audience. PAS's middle step is banned on therapy, crisis and medical surfaces
  (`safety.md`); use BAB there.
